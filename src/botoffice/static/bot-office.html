    Bot behavior
    
      
    Auto-continue
    
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
    A message from you or Stop ends the run, and a bot also stops when it says it is blocked or needs you. Continuations run quietly: the bot stays "Working" in the chat. Automatic turns are chat continuations, assignment steps and checks, and wake-ups; your own messages, Start and Reply, and routines are not counted. A bot over a cap waits instead of dropping work, and its chat and Needs you say which cap. Assignments also keep their own turn budget (20 turns by default). Chat-only bots (set in a bot's dialog) never continue.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Email notifications
    
      
    Bot Office can email you through its external watchdog (set up on the server; see the README). The mail carries only counts, bot names, job ids and a short reason, never message text. One mail per kind, and a digest when several arrive together. 
    
      
      
      
      
      
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Approvals
    
      
    Bots work on their own and rarely ask. Turn on the kinds of action you want done, but not without your yes. When Bot-Office recognises one, the bot pauses at that step and its chat shows an Approve / Deny card.
    
      
    This is defense in depth, not an inescapable boundary. A bot with full CLI access runs as the same Unix user as these checks, so it can get around them if it sets out to, and some actions will not be caught. Use GitHub rulesets and account permissions for what must never happen.
