Bot behavior

      
Auto-continue

      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
A message from you or Stop ends the run, and a bot also stops when it says it is blocked or needs you. Continuations run quietly: the bot stays "Working" in the chat. Automatic turns are chat continuations, assignment steps and checks, and wake-ups; your own messages, Start and Reply, and routines are not counted. A bot over a cap waits instead of dropping work, and its chat and Needs you say which cap. Assignments also keep their own turn budget (20 turns by default). Chat-only bots (set in a bot's dialog) never continue.

      

    

    

      
Approvals

      
Bots work on their own and rarely ask. Turn on the kinds of action you want done, but not without your yes. When a bot tries one, it pauses that one step and its chat shows an Approve / Deny card.
