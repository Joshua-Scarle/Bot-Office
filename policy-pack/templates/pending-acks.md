# Pending acknowledgements (template)

Copy this table into the "Pending acknowledgements" section of your WORKING.md. One row per major
report or update you sent Joshua. A row stays until he acknowledges it, acts on it, or declines it.
Silence is not an answer. Never put secrets, tokens, or personal contact details in a row.

How to use it:

1. When you send a major report, add a row the same turn (what you sent and the date/time).
2. Set a follow-up with `bot-task wake` so a still-open row comes back to you.
3. In your next report, list every open row in one line each and ask for what you need.
4. If Joshua messages you while a row is open, tell him which rows are open before anything else.
5. When he acknowledges, acts, or declines, mark the row closed with his words and the time.

| Sent (date, time ET) | What I sent (one line) | What I need from Joshua | Status | Last re-surfaced | Closed by (his words, time) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | open | | |

A routine, small or already-answered reply is not a major report and gets no row.
